How it works

Four steps. From scan to seated.

one

Guest scans the QR at your door

A simple QR code printed at the host stand. No app downloads, no logins. Their phone opens the queue page.

two

They join in under ten seconds

Name, phone, party size. They get a WhatsApp confirmation instantly with a live link to their spot.

three

You tap notify when their table is ready

One tap sends a WhatsApp ping and lets you call them. They reply "on my way" or "sorry" right in chat.

four

They walk in. You seat them.

No yelling names across the room. No clusters at the entrance. No standing around wondering when their turn is.
