Wait time formula

min(base + increment × (position − 1), cap)

Base — wait time shown to the guest at the front of the queue (position 1). Typical: 8 min on weekdays, 12–15 min on weekends.

Increment — how many minutes each additional position adds. Typical: 3 min for fast turnover, 5 min for slower seating.

Cap — the highest wait time the guest will ever see. Prevents scary numbers like "50 min" from putting guests off.

Quick start:

Guests see this as an estimate, not a guarantee. The formula trends conservative — actual seating usually happens faster as guests cancel or are bumped through quickly.
